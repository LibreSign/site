Allowing multiple people to sign an electronic document can accelerate approvals and improve collaboration across locations and time zones. The legal effect of the signatures still depends on the signing method, signer identity and applicable rules.

LibreSign can record and link each signature to a signer for verification and audit purposes. For example, representatives of different departments can review and sign a contract without organizing in-person meetings. A technical signature alone does not establish legal validity.

Using multi-signer workflows in LibreSign can reduce the time needed to complete contracts and agreements and improve collaboration, provided that organizations choose signing and identity-verification processes appropriate to their legal requirements.
